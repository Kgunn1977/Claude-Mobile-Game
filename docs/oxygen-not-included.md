# Oxygen Not Included

A trio of Duplicants — quirky, printed humanoid clones generated by a mysterious Printing Pod — awaken deep inside a vast, procedurally generated asteroid floating in space, with no memory of how they arrived. Guided by the player acting as a disembodied overseeing intelligence, they must dig into the surrounding rock and engineer a livable colony within the sealed, enclosed environment of the asteroid.

The setting is a self-contained underground world governed by simulated physics. Everything needed for life must be produced, managed, and recycled inside a closed system: breathable oxygen, drinkable water, food, power, and survivable temperatures, all while contending with toxic gases, germs, heat buildup, and the constant pressure of finite resources. The tone is lighthearted, cartoonish, and scientific — a hostile space-survival scenario presented with humor, where the real adversary is the relentless logic of thermodynamics and resource conservation.

## Production buildings & conversions

*Conversions are continuous mass flows in **kg/s** or **g/s** (rates are at full operation; several values are approximate and most also emit heat and consume power). Food outputs are measured in **kcal**.*

### Oxygen production
- **Oxygen Diffuser** — 550 g/s algae → 500 g/s oxygen
- **Electrolyzer** — 1000 g/s water → 888 g/s oxygen + 112 g/s hydrogen
- **Algae Terrarium** — water + CO2 + light → oxygen + polluted water (consumes algae)
- **Rust Deoxidizer** — rust + salt → oxygen + iron ore + chlorine
- **Carbon Skimmer** — 300 g/s CO2 + 1000 g/s water → 1000 g/s polluted water (CO2 removal)
- **Deodorizer** — polluted oxygen + filtration medium (clay/sand) → oxygen (+ clay)

### Water processing
- **Water Sieve** — 5 kg/s polluted water + 1 kg/s sand (filtration) → 5 kg/s clean water
- **Desalinator** — 5 kg/s salt water → 4.65 kg/s water + 350 g/s salt (brine: 5 kg/s → 3.5 kg/s water + 1.5 kg/s salt)
- **Algae Distiller** — 600 g/s slime → 200 g/s algae + 400 g/s polluted water
- **Ethanol Distiller** — lumber → ethanol + polluted dirt + CO2

### Metals & solids refinement
- **Metal Refinery** — 100 kg metal ore → 100 kg refined metal per cycle (dumps heat into coolant); also smelts alloys: iron + refined carbon + lime → steel; tungsten + niobium → thermium
- **Rock Crusher** — crushes materials: sandstone → sand; fossil → lime; salt → table salt; egg shells → lime; ore → small amount of refined metal
- **Kiln** — 100 kg clay + 25 kg coal → 100 kg ceramic (+ CO2)
- **Glass Forge** — 100 kg sand → 100 kg molten glass (cools into glass)

### Petrochemicals
- **Oil Refinery** — 10 kg/s crude oil → 5 kg/s petroleum (2:1) + natural gas (emitted)
- **Polymer Press** — 833 g/s petroleum → 333 g/s plastic (+ CO2 + steam; very high heat)
- **Molecular Forge** — refined metal / petroleum inputs → advanced materials (Super Coolant, Visco-Gel)
- **Oxylite Refinery** — oxygen + gold (catalyst) → oxylite (compressed solid oxygen)

### Agriculture support
- **Fertilizer Synthesizer** — dirt + phosphorite + polluted water → fertilizer + natural gas
- **Compost** — polluted dirt → dirt (slow; duplicant-tended)

### Food preparation
- **Microbe Musher** — 75 kg water + 75 kg dirt → 1 Mush Bar (also water + algae → Mush Bar)
- **Electric Grill / Gas Range** — cook raw produce into prepared meals, raising quality and kcal: meal lice → Liceloaf; bristle berry → Gristle Berry; mushroom → Fried Mushroom; sleet wheat grain + peppernut → Pepper Bread; meat → BBQ; pacu fillet → Cooked Fish; egg → Omelette
- **Apothecary** — balm lily + water → medicines / tonics

### Ranching (critter-based conversion)
- **Hatch** — eats rock/minerals → coal; **Smooth Hatch** — eats metal ore → refined metal
- **Drecko** — grazes (mealwood/balm lily) → reed fiber; **Glossy Drecko** → plastic
- **Critters generally** — produce meat, eggs, and egg shells when ranched

## Skills & traits

### Attributes
Each Duplicant has **11 attributes**, trained by performing related errands (each can rise up to +20 on top of modifiers). They govern how fast and how well a dupe does each job:

- Excavation (digging speed)
- Construction (build speed)
- Machinery (operating & tinkering with buildings)
- Athletics (movement speed; also cleaning)
- Strength (carrying capacity / delivery & supply)
- Cuisine (cooking speed & food quality)
- Agriculture (farming, harvesting)
- Ranching (critter grooming & wrangling)
- Learning (research speed)
- Creativity (art & decorating)
- Caring (medicine, doctoring, first aid)

### Interests
Each dupe is generated with **1–3 interests**, which give a starting attribute bonus (+7 / +3 / +1 — a hard floor), a +1 morale bonus for related skills, and faster XP in those areas.

### Skills (job tree)
Dupes earn skill points by gaining experience, then spend them in a **skill tree** to learn jobs. Each learned skill grants attribute bonuses and may unlock an ability — but **raises that dupe's morale requirement**. Branches include:

- Mining (harder digging) · Building (improved construction) · Tinkering (operate generators, tune-ups)
- Farming · Ranching · Cooking (unlocks better Electric Grill recipes)
- Research (man Super Computer, advanced & interstellar research)
- Art (decorating → fine art → masterworks) · Medicine (medical aid → advanced care)
- Suits (required to wear Atmo Suits) · Rocketry / Astronaut (pilot rockets)

### Traits
Each dupe is generated with a positive trait, a negative trait, a stress response, and an overjoy response (counts vary by generation; roughly ~15 positive and ~18 negative traits exist).

- **Positive (examples):** Diver's Lungs (−25 g/s oxygen use) · Buff (+5 Strength) · Iron Gut (no food poisoning) · Germ Resistant · Simple Tastes (happy with any food) · Foodie (+3 Cuisine) · Quick Learner · Caregiver · Interior Decorator
- **Negative (examples):** Mouth Breather (extra oxygen use) · Flatulent (emits natural gas) · Narcoleptic · Loud Sleeper · Small Bladder · Bottomless Stomach (eats more) · Anemic (−Athletics) · Noodle Arms (−Strength)
- **Job-disabling negatives:** Unconstructive (can't build) · Uncultured (can't do art) · Yokel (can't research) · Squeamish (can't give medical aid) · Gastrophobia (can't cook) · Pacifist (can't fight)

### Stress responses (one per dupe, at high stress)
- Destructive (smashes/deconstructs buildings)
- Binge Eater (devours stored food)
- Ugly Crier (cries; lowers nearby morale)
- Vomiter (vomits polluted water)

### Overjoy responses (at maximum morale)
- Balloon Artist · Sticker Bomber · Super Productive

## Map & environment

### Map
- A single, bounded, procedurally generated **asteroid**, shown as a 2D vertical cross-section: open Space at the top, indestructible Neutronium walling the edges. (Base game = one asteroid.)
- Map-gen variables: **seed**; **difficulty preset** (Survival / No Sweat, or custom settings for stress, calories, durability, morale, immunity); **World/Asteroid Traits** (modifiers) such as Geoactive (+geysers) / Geodormant (−geysers), Metal Rich / Metal Poor, Boulders, Slime Molds, Subsurface Ocean, Volcanoes, Irregular Oil, Frozen Core.

### Biomes
Temperate (**Sandstone — starting biome**) · Forest · Swamp (Marsh) · Caustic (Jungle) · Frozen (Tundra) · Ocean · Rust · Oil · Magma (Volcanic) · Space (surface) · plus resource-poor Barren pockets. Each biome has its own native temperature band, gases, liquids, minerals, plants, and critters — and **biomes do not regulate their own temperature** (a frozen biome only stays cold while its wild Wheezeworts remain).

### Per-tile simulated variables (the heart of the game)
- **Temperature** — full heat simulation: conduction between materials, phase changes, and building overheating
- **Atmosphere** — each tile holds one gas; gases layer by density; pressure matters (vacuum = no breathing; very high pressure pops eardrums → stress)
- **Liquids** — each tile holds one liquid; gravity-driven flow; flooding and drowning risk
- **Germs / disease** — tracked in gases, liquids, and on surfaces (e.g. Slimelung, Food Poisoning)
- **Decor** — per-tile value affecting Duplicant morale
- **Light** — affects some plants and gives a small morale bonus
- **Rooms** — enclosed spaces of the right size/contents grant bonuses (Bedroom, Mess Hall, Great Hall, Latrine, Hospital, etc.)

### Geysers & vents (renewable element sources; ~12 buried per map)
- **Water / steam:** Water Geyser · Cool Steam Vent · Steam Vent · Cool Slush Geyser · Salt Water Geyser · Cool Salt Slush Geyser · Polluted Water Vent
- **Gases:** Natural Gas Geyser · Hydrogen Vent · Chlorine Gas Vent · Carbon Dioxide Geyser / Vent · Infectious Polluted Oxygen Vent
- **Liquids / metals:** Leaky Oil Fissure · Minor Volcano · Volcano · metal volcanoes (Copper, Iron, Gold)

### Time & hazards
- Time is measured in **Cycles** (~600 seconds = one day/night cycle); day/night affects sleep schedules, some plants, and solar panels
- **Meteor showers** strike the Space-biome surface (regolith and metal meteors) — the closest thing to "weather"
- Core hazards: heat buildup, toxic/unbreathable gases, germs, drowning, entombment, and starvation

## Jobs / tasks / work / roles

### Model
Duplicants automatically pick up **errands** based on two overlapping systems. The **Priority panel** sets, per duplicant per errand category, a priority of **1–9** (default 5) or *disabled*, plus a *Very High / Top* tier; a dupe does its highest-priority available errand first. The **building sub-priority overlay** (1–9) breaks ties between buildings offering the same errand. A **Top Priority** flag makes dupes drop everything for one task, and **Red Alert** puts the whole colony into emergency mode (ignoring even personal needs). Normally dupes always interrupt work to handle eat/sleep/bathroom needs.

**Schedules** divide each cycle into blocks (Work, Downtime, Sleep, Bathtime) per dupe or group. Specialization comes from the **Skill tree** (jobs that boost attributes but raise the dupe's morale requirement). Some errands require a learned skill (e.g. Decorate needs Art Fundamentals; cooking on an Electric Grill needs Grilling), and traits can disable errands (e.g. Uncultured → no Decorate).

### Errand categories (15)
- Combat
- Build (construction)
- Care (medicine / doctoring)
- Cook
- Decorate (art)
- Dig (excavation)
- Farm (botany)
- Life Support
- Operate (machinery / tinkering)
- Ranching (husbandry)
- Research
- Storage (sweep into bins)
- Supply (deliver materials)
- Tidy (clean / mop / disinfect)
- Toggle (flip switches, doors, valves)

## Duplicant needs & vitals

Each Duplicant continuously tracks: **Breath/Oxygen** (consumes ~100 g/s O₂, exhales CO₂; suffocates without breathable air), **Calories** (eats ~1000 kcal/cycle from stored food; starves if empty), **Bladder** (fills over a cycle → must use a toilet/lavatory, producing polluted water; accidents if ignored), **Sleep** (must rest each cycle), and **Health** (HP, lost to heat, cold, bad gas, scalding/freezing liquid, and combat). Layered on top are **Decor expectations** and **Stress/Morale**.

## Morale & stress

- Each dupe has **Morale** (raised by food quality, decor, recreation, rooms/Great Hall, downtime, massages) measured against a **Morale Expectation** that **rises with every Skill learned**.
- If morale ≥ expectation, **Stress** falls; if morale < expectation, stress climbs (0–100%). High stress triggers the dupe's **stress response** — Destructive, Binge Eater, Ugly Crier, or Vomiter. Maxed morale can trigger an **Overjoy reaction**.

## Health, germs & disease

- HP drops from temperature extremes, unbreathable gas, scalding/freezing liquids, and combat; injured dupes get debuffs and may need a **Med Bed / Disease Clinic** and **Care** (medicine) errands.
- **Germs** are tracked per-tile in gases, liquids, and on surfaces. The two main diseases: **Food Poisoning** (unwashed hands/contaminated food → vomiting, lost calories) and **Slimelung** (polluted oxygen in slime/caustic biomes → coughing, impaired breathing). The oil biome's **Sporechid** emits zombie spores.
- **Defenses:** sinks / wash basins / hand sanitizers at bathrooms and kitchens, sealing off contaminated biomes (atmo suits), **chlorine rooms** (kill germs), and innate germ resistance/immunity.

## Temperature & heat management

- The **defining late-game challenge.** Everything has a temperature and exchanges heat: machines have **overheat limits** and break if too hot, plants and dupes have comfort ranges, and food spoils unless kept cold.
- **Tools:** Insulated Tiles/Insulation and vacuum gaps to block heat; **Wheezeworts** (passive cooling, consume phosphorite); and the core **heat-deletion loop** — a **Thermo Aquatuner** cools a liquid while dumping its heat into a sealed steam room, where a **Steam Turbine** converts that heat into power, permanently removing it. Cold biomes and chlorine/hydrogen pockets serve as heat sinks.

## Power

- An electrical grid: **generators → wires / conductive wires → batteries** (incl. Smart Battery automation) → consumers, with **wattage limits** (wires overload and break above their rating) and **transformers** to segment circuits.
- **Generators:** Manual Generator (dupe-powered), Coal, Wood (via Ethanol), Hydrogen, Natural Gas, Petroleum, Solar Panel, and Steam Turbine. Most emit heat and/or exhaust gas that must itself be managed.

## Research & technology

- A tech tree unlocked at research buildings: the **Research Station** (early tiers) → **Super Computer** (advanced, consumes water) → interstellar/telescope-based research (data banks) for the highest tiers.
- A dupe operating the station generates research points, unlocking buildings and systems (oxygen, plumbing, power, refinement, automation, space, etc.).

## Base building & non-production buildings

- Built from mined materials: **Tiles** (walls/floors), specialized tiles (Insulated, Airflow, Mesh), **Ladders, Fire Poles, Doors**, and the liquid/gas **pipes and wires** threaded through them.
- **Quality-of-life buildings** drive morale: Cots/Beds, Mess Hall + Mess Tables, Latrines/Lavatories + sinks, Recreation (Arcade, Espresso Machine, Water Cooler), Great Hall, Med Beds, and decor (sculptures, paintings). Enclosed **rooms** of the right size/contents grant room bonuses.

## Farming & ranching

- Plants grow wild or in **Farm Tiles / Hydroponic Tiles / Planter Boxes**; domestic crops need the right **temperature, atmosphere, lighting, irrigation, and fertilizer**, and each species has specific needs (e.g. Bristle Blossom needs light; Sleet Wheat needs cold).
- **Ranching:** critters (Hatch, Drecko, Pacu, Pip, and variants) live in stables, are **groomed/wrangled** by a Rancher, lay eggs, and convert inputs to useful outputs (coal, refined metal, reed fiber, plastic, meat, eggs). Critters have lifecycles, tameness, and population limits.

## Atmo suits & hazard traversal

- Protective equipment for hostile zones: the **Atmo Suit** (worn at a Suit Checkpoint/Dock; supplies oxygen and shields from heat, cold, and toxic gas) and the simpler **Oxygen Mask** (oxygen only).
- Suits require the **Suit-wearing skill** and ongoing oxygen refill, and they **slow the dupe down** — the price of safely exploring caustic, oil, and space biomes.

## Combat

- Combat is **minimal**: there are **no raids, wars, or enemy factions**. The only hostiles are aggressive critters (e.g. a disturbed **Pokeshell**).
- Dupes use the **Combat** errand to attack/kill critters — for meat or to clear a threat. There are no weapons, armor tiers, or armies; the real "enemy" is the environment itself (heat, gas, germs).

## Time & cycles

- Time is measured in **Cycles** (~600 seconds = one day/night). Day/night governs sleep schedules, solar power, and some plants.
- **Game speed:** pause + fast-forward. Goals are largely **open-ended/self-set** (survive, grow, reach space) — there's no single mandated win, though reaching space/launching is the de facto endgame.

## Logistics & storage

- Loose materials are moved by **Supply / Storage errands**: a dupe carries debris to storage by hand (capacity scales with **Strength**); the **Sweep** tool flags scattered material for pickup.
- **Storage buildings** — Storage Bin / **Storage Compactor** (solids), **Ration Box** and powered **Refrigerator** (food — the fridge slows spoilage), **Liquid Reservoir** and **Gas Reservoir** (bulk fluids), and **Smart Storage** (with automation output). Each has a per-element **filter** (which materials it accepts) and a **sub-priority** (where dupes prefer to deposit).
- **Spoilage & preservation** — food rots in warm, oxygen-rich conditions; players preserve it by refrigeration, by storing it in **sterile / cold / vacuum / CO₂ environments**, or by cooking it into longer-lasting dishes.
- *(ONI also has an automated solid-transport layer — conveyor rails, loaders, and receptacles — but that belongs to the engineering/automation systems we're intentionally not documenting.)*

## Balancing (availability · production · consumption)

### Overarching philosophy
ONI is balanced as a **near-closed thermodynamic system**. Mass is almost always **conserved** (conversions preserve it; true creation/deletion is rare), so the colony is a web of loops in which every output is another loop's input. The thing that *isn't* conserved is **heat** — it's produced almost everywhere and rarely destroyed — so **heat is the entropy that slowly accumulates and becomes the real difficulty curve**. Early game is comfortable; left alone, the colony cooks itself.

### Availability — how resources enter
- **Finite, non-renewing:** mined ores, algae, slime, and the magma/cold reservoirs. Algae-based oxygen is a finite *crutch* the player must outgrow.
- **Renewable, rate-limited:** **geysers and vents** — the backbone of the late economy. Every map guarantees at least **two cool steam vents** (water → oxygen) and **one natural gas vent** (power). Each geyser has an **eruption period** (active %) and a long **dormancy cycle** (25–225 cycles, active 40–80% of the time), so its *average* output is capped — one geyser sustains roughly **10–30 dupes**. Geysers over-pressurize and stall if not vented; Geotuners boost them.
- **Renewable, engineered:** **farming** (crops with strict temperature/atmosphere/irrigation/light needs) and **ranching** (critters convert inputs into coal, refined metal, plastic, reed fiber, meat, eggs).

### Production — the conversion economy
- Production is **continuous mass-flow** (g/s, kg/s), and nearly every building carries **three simultaneous costs: power, labor (a dupe operating it), and heat output.** E.g. an Electrolyzer turns 1,000 g/s water into 888 g/s oxygen + 112 g/s hydrogen *plus heat*; refineries dump large heat into coolant.
- The intended arc is building **self-sustaining loops** (the classic SPOM: electrolyzer + hydrogen generator powering itself), so the balance question is rarely "do I have the input?" but "**can I handle the byproducts and the heat?**"
- **Duplicant skills/attributes** raise speed, but every learned skill **raises that dupe's morale requirement** — capability has an ongoing morale cost.

### Consumption — the per-capita drains
- **Oxygen:** ~**100 g/s** per dupe (≈ 1 kg/s water per 8 dupes via electrolysis).
- **Calories:** ~**1,000 kcal/cycle** per dupe; food spoils unless kept cold/sterile/vacuum.
- **Bladder/waste:** each dupe fills a bladder per cycle, *producing* polluted water (a resource, if recycled).
- **Power:** consumers draw continuously against generation; wires overload above their wattage rating; batteries buffer.
- **Stress:** climbs whenever morale < expectation — a continuous drain that triggers stress responses if unmanaged.

### Dominant pressure
The lethal curve is **heat death**: machines, refineries, and even oxygen production steadily raise base temperature until plants die, food spoils, and buildings overheat. The mid-to-late game is fundamentally about **deleting or exporting heat** (aquatuner + steam turbine; dumping into cold biomes or space). Population is self-limiting — each new dupe adds fixed oxygen/calorie/heat load — so growth is gated by your loops, not by any threat system.
