# Vision

*The creative foundation for the game. Names and specific places intentionally left out — this is the premise in general form. Sits alongside the research docs and tech-stack.md.*

## Premise

The national government has collapsed after the destruction of the economic system, and foreign powers move in to fight over the former nation's natural resources. An ordinary family man, desperate to protect the people he loves, flees into the wilderness.

After struggling alone — and losing a loved one to the harsh elements — he realizes that survival isn't possible in isolation: his family will need other people and shared resources to make it. He leads a group of refugees deep into the forest to build a safe, secure community for their families and friends.

The new colony fights to survive against hunger, sickness, and hostile outsiders. And when rumors of a new government forming elsewhere turn out to be something far darker than the colonists hoped, they're forced to confront a permanent truth: the world has changed forever.

## Core themes

- **Survival through community** — one person can't make it alone; the colony is the answer to the wilderness.
- **Refuge and rebuilding** — ordinary people creating safety and order out of collapse.
- **External pressure** — hunger and sickness from within; hostile outsiders (and a looming larger power) from without.
- **A changed world** — no return to the old normal; the hope of "rescue" or restored order curdles into a new, harder reality.

## Tone

Grounded, near-future post-collapse survival. Human-scale and personal (it starts with one family), expanding into a community under constant pressure. Hopeful in spirit but unsentimental about the stakes.
